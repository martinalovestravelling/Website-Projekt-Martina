PHASE 01 — ASSESSPHASE 01 — ASSESS

00

Assessment & Setup

Organizational and participant assessment, goals, framework conditions and maturity level. The participant assessment is for orientation only — everyone goes through the same exercises, learning together as a team. Organisations- und Teilnehmer-Assessment, Ziele, Rahmenbedingungen und Reifegrad. Das Teilnehmer-Assessment dient nur zur Orientierung — alle durchlaufen dieselben Übungen und lernen gemeinsam als Team.

PHASE 02 — LEARNPHASE 02 — LEARN

01

AI Fundamentals

What is AI? Generative AI, LLMs, RAG, agents, automation — opportunities and limits. Goal: a shared understanding. Was ist KI? Generative AI, LLMs, RAG, Agents, Automatisierung — Chancen und Grenzen. Ziel: ein gemeinsames Verständnis.

02

Prompting Fundamentals

Role, context, audience, instruction, constraints, output format, examples, verification. Good AI use starts before the prompt. Role, Context, Audience, Instruction, Constraints, Output Format, Examples, Verification. Gute KI-Nutzung beginnt vor dem eigentlichen Prompt.

03

AI Tools & Capabilities

Not a tool list: from the problem to the matching capability — research, knowledge retrieval, document analysis, content generation, classification, decision support, workflow automation, agents. Keine reine Tool-Liste: vom Problem zur passenden Capability — Research, Knowledge Retrieval, Document Analysis, Content Generation, Classification, Decision Support, Workflow Automation, Agents.

PHASE 03 — PRACTICEPHASE 03 — PRACTICE

04

Prompting Practice Lab

Core exercise "From Information to Useful Output", following Think → Contextualize → Prompt → Verify. Kernübung „From Information to Useful Output" nach dem Prinzip Think → Contextualize → Prompt → Verify.

PHASE 04 — APPLYPHASE 04 — APPLY

05

Business Problem & Process

Strategic objective → business problem → process → pain point → root cause → AI opportunity. Thinking AI from the business problem. Strategic Objective → Business Problem → Process → Pain Point → Root Cause → AI Opportunity. KI vom Business Problem aus denken.

06

Use Case Discovery

AI opportunity hunt, identifying and clustering use cases, teamwork, formulating business outcomes. AI Opportunity Hunt, Use Cases identifizieren und clustern, Teamarbeit, Business Outcomes formulieren.

PHASE 05 — VALIDATEPHASE 05 — VALIDATE

07

Prioritization & Validation

Assessment along business value, feasibility, risk and adoption. Bewertung entlang Business Value, Feasibility, Risk und Adoption.

08

Business Case & Value

Quantifying benefits, costs, effort, time-to-value, KPIs and a decision paper. Time saved does not automatically mean cost saved. Nutzen, Kosten, Aufwand, Time-to-Value, KPIs und Entscheidungsvorlage quantifizieren. Time saved bedeutet nicht automatisch Cost Saved.

PHASE 06 — IMPLEMENTPHASE 06 — IMPLEMENT

09

Solution Design & Prototype

Prompt, prompt chain, workflow, RAG, agent, integration — use the simplest AI solution that reliably solves the business problem. Prompt, Prompt Chain, Workflow, RAG, Agent, Integration — die einfachste KI-Lösung nutzen, die das Business Problem zuverlässig löst.

PHASE 07 — SUSTAIN & SCALEPHASE 07 — SUSTAIN & SCALE

10

Change, Sustain & Scale

Awareness → understanding → involvement → capability → adoption → reinforcement, communication plan, sustain & scale KPIs, AI capability portfolio. Change starts on day one. Awareness → Understanding → Involvement → Capability → Adoption → Reinforcement, Kommunikationsplan, Sustain-&-Scale-KPIs, AI Capability Portfolio. Change beginnt am ersten Tag.
