# Briefing: Professional Profile Website

## 1. Purpose

A public, one-page website presenting a professional profile: short bio, industries, experience, skills, certifications and international experience. The site is meant to give a clear, well-structured overview of expertise. It is not a full CV.

## 2. Privacy Rules (mandatory)

The website must **not** contain any personal data. In particular, do not include:

- Name, photo, date of birth
- Phone number, email address, postal address, place of residence
- Names of employers, clients or universities
- References to specific job applications or target companies

Employers are described only by their industry or type (e.g. "global management consultancy").

## 3. Language Requirements

- **Bilingual: English (primary) and German (secondary).**
- English is the default language and is always shown first.
- Two acceptable display options (to be decided during the build):
  - **Option A, Language switch:** a toggle (EN / DE) that switches the whole page.
  - **Option B, Stacked translation:** the German text appears directly below each English text, smaller and in grey.
- All content below is provided in both languages. Tool names, certification names and established technical terms remain in English in both versions.
